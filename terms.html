Terms of guest booking

Updated 1 November 2026

Guest meeting booking

A paid guest booking is for the NIA Hyderabad meeting shown at checkout. The guest fee is ₹2,600, inclusive of applicable taxes and dinner, unless the checkout clearly states otherwise. Membership is separate from a guest booking.

Confirmation and attendance

A booking is confirmed only after Razorpay reports a successful payment and NIA Hyderabad sends confirmation. Guests must provide accurate contact details and follow reasonable venue and event instructions.

Membership and category availability

Attending as a guest does not create a membership, guarantee a membership offer, or reserve a business category. NIA Hyderabad may discuss category availability and membership separately.

Changes or cancellation by NIA

NIA Hyderabad may change event logistics where reasonably necessary. If NIA cancels an event, the guest may choose a full refund or a pass to the next eligible NIA Hyderabad event.

Contact

For booking support, contact NIA Hyderabad on +91 7997994493.
