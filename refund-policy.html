Cancellation and refund policy

Updated 1 November 2026

Guest cancellation

Guest booking cancellations are non-refundable.

If NIA cancels

If NIA Hyderabad cancels the event, the guest may choose either a full refund or a pass to the next eligible NIA Hyderabad event.

Payment processing

Payments are processed by Razorpay. Refunds approved by NIA Hyderabad are returned through the original payment method where possible and may take time to appear, depending on the payment provider or bank.

Help with a booking

Contact NIA Hyderabad with the guest name, mobile number and payment reference on +91 7997994493.
