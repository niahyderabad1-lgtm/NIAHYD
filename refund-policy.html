Cancellation and refund policy

Updated 1 November 2026

Guest cancellation and rescheduling

Guest booking cancellations are non-refundable. If the guest informs NIA Hyderabad at least one day before the scheduled meeting starts, the booking can be moved to the next NIA Hyderabad meeting, subject to availability.

Payment processing

Payments are processed by Razorpay. No payment-card details are collected by NIA Hyderabad.

Help with a booking

Contact NIA Hyderabad with the guest name, mobile number and payment reference on +91 7997994493.
