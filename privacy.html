Privacy policy

Updated 7 October 2026

This policy covers NIA Hyderabad’s visitor registration page, staff CRM and WhatsApp messaging integration.

Information we use

We collect details you provide, including your name, phone number, email, business name, category and years in business, along with consent and campaign-source information. For WhatsApp conversations we may store message content, sender identifiers, timestamps and delivery or read status. Visitor bookings may include payment references and payment status.

Purpose and access

We use this information to answer enquiries, manage event bookings, schedule follow-up and communicate with you. Marketing broadcasts are intended only for recipients with recorded opt-in permission. Registration is not automatic consent to marketing broadcasts. CRM records are restricted to authorised NIA staff.

Service providers

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Supabase provides CRM storage and backend services. Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform processes WhatsApp messages and delivery events. Razorpay processes payments when payment collection is enabled. We do not collect payment card details in the CRM. These providers process information under their own applicable policies and may process data outside your country.

Retention and security

We retain information as needed for enquiries, business follow-up, bookings and applicable recordkeeping obligations. Access controls protect CRM records. No online service can guarantee absolute security.
