GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions, answered.

Who is NIA Hyderabad for?

Business owners and entrepreneurs who value long-term relationships, contribute to a trusted community and want to explore opportunities to grow.

What happens after I enquire?

The Hyderabad team reviews your business details, contacts you and helps you explore a visitor meeting or membership discussion. An enquiry alone does not reserve an event seat.

Is this an annual membership registration?

No. This page is for visiting a meeting and exploring NIA. Membership is a separate decision.

Can I talk to someone before paying?

Yes. You can share an enquiry with the Hyderabad team on WhatsApp. This does not reserve a visitor seat.

Is my business category available?

NIA follows a one-seat-per-category model. The Hyderabad team can confirm availability and visitor eligibility for your business.

What does the visitor fee include?

The ₹2,600 visitor fee includes taxes and dinner. Cancellation and refund terms will be confirmed before paid registrations open.
